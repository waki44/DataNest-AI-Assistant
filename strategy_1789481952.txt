# 2026 Desktop App Growth Strategy

## Key Objectives
1. **User Acquisition**: Expand user base by 40% through targeted digital marketing and strategic partnerships.
2. **Product Innovation**: Integrate advanced AI workflows, enhanced offline capabilities, and seamless cross-platform sync.
3. **User Retention**: Increase 30-day retention rate to 65% via personalized onboarding and regular feature updates.
4. **Monetization**: Introduce tier-based enterprise subscriptions and premium add-ons.

## Execution Plan
- **Q1**: Onboarding redesign & performance optimization.
- **Q2**: AI features rollout & localized community outreach.
- **Q3**: Enterprise tier launch & B2B integration capabilities.
- **Q4**: Annual strategy review and user feedback synthesis.
